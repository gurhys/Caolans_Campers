Water Needs

From a simple hand pump and jerry can to a full plumbed system with a hot water boiler — Caolán builds water systems that actually work for the life you're living.

More Than a Tap

Built Around How
You Actually Live

A water system needs to match how you use the van. If you're wild camping for weeks at a time, you need a decent tank and efficient pump. If you're doing weekend trips and always near a campsite, something simpler might be all that's needed. Caolán doesn't upsell you on plumbing you don't need.

He'll talk through how you plan to use the van — cooking, washing up, showering — and spec a system that makes sense. Then he'll fit it so everything is accessible, pipework is properly clipped and supported, and there are no hidden fittings buried behind panelling that you can't reach if something leaks.

"The test of a good water system is when something eventually needs attention. Everything's accessible. Nothing's a mystery."

System Options

Basic

Simple Cold Supply

A compact tank, 12V pump, and a tap. Straightforward, reliable, and fits under a bed or inside a bench. Right for weekend use or builds where simplicity is the priority.

  • 10–30L tank (positioned to suit your layout)
  • 12V diaphragm or submersible pump
  • Single cold tap
  • Drain valve and grey water container

Standard

Cold Supply & Grey Water

A properly plumbed system with a larger tank, pressure-regulated pump, and a grey water tank to contain waste correctly. The right choice for longer trips or anyone living in the van regularly.

  • 30–60L fresh water tank
  • Pressure pump with accumulator
  • Kitchen tap with strainer
  • Dedicated grey water tank
  • Tank level gauge

Full System

Hot & Cold Plumbing

Hot and cold taps, an external shower hookup, and a water heater. What you need for full-time van life or if you want a genuinely comfortable setup that doesn't feel like camping.

  • 60–100L+ fresh water tank
  • Hot & cold pressure supply
  • Diesel or electric water heater
  • External shower point
  • Grey water tank with level indicator

Hot Water Options

Hot water in a van is a genuine comfort upgrade — washing up in cold water every night gets old quickly. Caolán installs a few different systems depending on your setup and budget.

Diesel Water Heater (Webasto / Eberspächer)

The premium choice. Heats water from diesel — same fuel as your space heater if you have one. Quiet, reliable, and works when it's -5° outside. Caolán's recommendation for full-timers.

Calorifier / Heat Exchanger

Uses the van's engine cooling system to heat water as you drive. Free hot water every time you arrive at your destination — no extra fuel, no extra power draw.

Electric Boiler (Hookup)

A small 240V water heater that works on site hookup. Lowest cost to install, perfectly adequate if you're mostly on campsites.
