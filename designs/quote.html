Yes — Caolán converts vans that you already own or have sourced yourself. He doesn't supply the base vehicle, but he can advise on what to look for before you buy. Things like roof height, wheelbase, existing rust, and whether the van is suitable for the layout you have in mind.

Most people buy from Done Deal, AutoTrader Ireland, or local dealers. Main dealer used vans are often the safest bet — full service history and less likely to have hidden rust. If you're not sure whether a van you're looking at is suitable, get in touch with Caolán before you commit — a five-minute conversation can save you from a bad purchase.

For a full live-in build, a long-wheelbase high-roof van is strongly recommended — standing room and enough length for a fixed bed, kitchen, and storage without compromise. Ford Transit LWB, VW Crafter, and Mercedes Sprinter are the most popular choices. For weekends away or a simple sleep setup, a medium-wheelbase van works well and is easier to drive daily.
