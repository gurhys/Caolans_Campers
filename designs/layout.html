Layout & Furniture

No two layouts are the same because no two people use a van the same way. Caolán designs and builds everything from scratch — nothing flat-packed, nothing generic.

Built to Fit Your Life

Your Van.
Your Layout.

The layout is the most personal part of any build. A solo surfer needs something completely different from a couple travelling together, or a remote worker who needs a proper desk setup. Before Caolán picks up a saw, he takes the time to understand exactly how you'll be using the van — and designs around that, not around what's easiest to build.

Every piece of furniture is custom made — sized to fit the van's curves and contours, making use of every available centimetre. Drawer slides, hinges, and hardware are all quality fittings. The woodwork is finished to a standard Caolán would be happy with in his own van, because he was.

"I designed my own layout three times before I was happy with it. That obsession over detail is what you get in your build."

Common Layouts

These are starting points — Caolán will adapt any of these to your van's specific dimensions and your specific needs.

Most Popular

Fixed Rear Bed

Permanent bed at the back — you make it once and you're done. Kitchen or seating in the mid-section, storage underneath. Simple, functional, and easy to live with.

Best for: Solo travellers, couples, full-timers

Space Saving

Convertible Bed

Seating by day, bed by night. Maximises usable space during the day — better for people who spend a lot of time inside, or who need the van floor clear for gear or dogs.

Best for: Weekend use, van with a dog, flexible living

Best for Two

Island / Side Bed

Bed runs lengthwise on one side, kitchen on the other. You can walk around three sides of the bed — a proper comfort upgrade that works really well in a longer van.

Best for: Couples, LWB vans, regular travel

Work & Travel

Office / Desk Build

A permanent desk or work surface that doesn't feel like an afterthought. Monitor mounts, cable management, proper chair space — Caolán builds for remote workers who actually need to work.

Best for: Remote workers, digital nomads

Storage. Lots of It.

Van life without smart storage is van chaos. Caolán thinks storage into every build from the start — not as an afterthought once the bed and kitchen are in. Under-bed drawers, overhead lockers, garage space under the floor, side pockets — every centimetre counted.

He builds drawers that open smoothly and stay shut on a bumpy road. Overhead lockers that are actually accessible standing up. Under-floor storage that doesn't require moving the entire contents of the van to get to your gear.

Under-Bed Drawers

Full-extension drawer slides, properly mounted. Every centimetre under the bed put to work.

Overhead Lockers

Sized and positioned so you can actually reach them, with proper latches that won't fly open on the road.

Garage Space

Dedicated space for wetsuits, bikes, boards, boots — accessible from the rear doors without disturbing the living area.

Handbuilt Cabinetry

Every cabinet in a Caolán build is made to measure — no off-the-shelf flat-pack units trimmed to fit. Hardwood-faced ply, solid wood edging, quality hardware. It looks right, fits right, and lasts.

Worktops are typically solid oak, butcher block, or composite — again, chosen to match the finish you're going for, not just what happens to be in the workshop.

Caolán can show you what previous builds looked like before you commit, so you know exactly the quality you're getting.
