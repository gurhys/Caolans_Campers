From Bare Metal to the Road

Full Van Conversion

A complete conversion designed around you — layout, electrics, heating, water, windows, and finishings. One person, start to finish, no subcontractors.

The Full Package

Everything Under
One Roof

A full conversion is the most personal thing you can put in a van. Caolán handles every part of the build himself — no passing off the electrics to one person and the joinery to another. That means every decision is joined up, every system works together, and nothing gets lost in translation between trades.

He starts with a proper conversation about how you'll actually use the van. Weekend surfer or full-time remote worker? Solo or with a partner? Need a proper bed or happy with a fold-down? The answers shape everything that follows.

"I don't start building until I understand your life. The van has to fit around you — not the other way around."
Start Your Build

How It Works

Initial Consultation

A proper conversation — in person, on a call, or over WhatsApp. Caolán gets to know how you travel, what you need, and what your budget looks like. No pressure, no sales pitch.

Design & Quote

Based on your brief, he puts together a layout plan and a detailed quote. Every component is itemised — you know exactly what you're paying for and why.

Parts Sourced

Caolán sources quality components — not the cheapest thing on the shelf, but value-for-money parts he's used before and trusts. Lead times are agreed upfront.

The Build

Work is carried out at his workshop or on location. You're kept updated throughout — progress photos, questions answered, no surprises at handover.

Sign-Off & Handover

Every system is tested and commissioned before handover. Caolán walks you through everything so you know how it all works — and what to do if something ever needs attention.

Ongoing Support

The job doesn't end at handover. Caolán is available if you ever have questions, need a tweak, or run into something unexpected on the road.

What's Included

Layout & Furniture

Custom floor plan, bespoke joinery, bed, kitchen units, seating, and storage built to fit your van's exact dimensions.

Power & Electrics

Solar panels, leisure battery, 12V circuits, USB points, lighting, and 240V inverter where needed. Engineered and documented.

Heating

Diesel or Chinese heater installed and commissioned. Properly routed exhaust, fuel feed, and controls — warm and safe.

Water System

Fresh and grey water tanks, 12V pump, sink, and hot water option. Clean running water wherever you are.

Skylights & Windows

Roof vents, fan lights, and side windows cut and sealed properly. Light, ventilation, and a view — without leaks.

Interior Finishings

Wall cladding, ceiling lining, flooring, and upholstery. The details that make it feel like somewhere you actually want to be.
