Diesel Air Heaters

Stay Warm in Ireland

Irish weather doesn't do warm winters. A properly installed diesel heater changes everything — warm van in minutes, whatever the weather outside.

Why Diesel Heating

The Right Call for
Year-Round Van Life

In a country where you can get four seasons in one afternoon, a quality heating system isn't optional — it's what separates a useable van from one that gets parked up from October to March. Diesel heaters are the gold standard for campervan use: they're fuel-efficient, reliable, and work even when it's proper Baltic outside.

Caolán sources and installs top-range units — properly, with the fuel line routed safely, the combustion air intake and exhaust positioned correctly, and the controller wired so it's actually easy to use.

"A badly fitted heater is a carbon monoxide risk. There's no cutting corners on this one."

The Installation

01

Position & Plan

Caolán plans the heater, fuel tank tap, intake and exhaust positions before touching anything — safety first, always.

02

Fuel & Mounting

Fuel line routed safely away from heat, properly clipped and supported. Heater body mounted solidly to the van structure.

03

Ducting & Outlets

Hot air ducted where it's actually needed — under the bed, into the living space — not just dumped behind a panel.

04

Wire & Commission

Wired directly to the battery with a proper fuse. Commissioned, run-tested, and handed over with the controller fully explained.
