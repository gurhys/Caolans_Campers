Overall Progress62% — Step 4 of 8

1 — Strip & Preparation

Van fully stripped. Rust found on rear sill — treated and sealed before it could spread. Sound deadening applied to floor, wheel arches, and inner panels. Solid start.

✓ Completed 12 May 2026

2 — Insulation

Spray foam into all the ribs, voids, and awkward corners. Rigid Celotex board on the main walls and floor. Thinsulate in the door cards and cab divider. Not a cold bridge left in the place.

✓ Completed 19 May 2026

3 — Electrical Rough-In

All cable runs done before the cladding goes on — this is the important part. Every cable is rated to its load, routed away from heat sources, and documented. You'll get the wiring diagram at handover.

✓ Completed 27 May 2026

4 — Framing & Structure

The bones of the layout are taking shape. Bed frame is built, overhead locker structure is in, and the kitchen unit carcasses are done. Taking the time to get everything square before the cladding goes on.

⟳ Started 2 Jun 2026 · In progress

Webasto air heater and hot water unit both on-site and ready to fit. Water plumbing will follow immediately after.

7

7 — Final Fit-Out & Electrical Commissioning

Furniture doors, fridge, hob, all lighting, battery monitor, and full system test.
