Why It Matters

The Backbone of Every Build

A van's electrical system is the one thing you really don't want to get wrong. Undersized cables, poorly fused circuits, and dodgy connections are a fire risk — full stop. Caolán designs every system from scratch, sized to your actual usage, not a generic template.

As a qualified engineer, he understands load calculations, cable ratings, and fault protection in a way most conversion builders simply don't. Every circuit is fused correctly, every connection is solid, and the whole system is documented so you know exactly what's in your van.
